# _Good Enough UX_ checklist

A lightweight checklist for improving usability without needing a full UX team or process. Use this before shipping features or changes — small adjustments here can prevent support issues, user frustration, and rework.


## 🧭 Clarity & Language

☐ Are all buttons and links clear about **what they do**?  
> _"Save draft" is clearer than "Submit" or "Continue"_

☐ Are error messages helpful and **actionable**?  
>_Avoid: “Something went wrong.” Use: “Couldn’t save. Check your connection and try again.”_

☐ Are you avoiding jargon or internal terms users may not know?

☐ Is the copy **consistent** with other parts of the product?  
>_e.g., If you use “project” elsewhere, don’t call it a “workspace” here._


## 🧠 Predictability & Flow

☐ Does the page or screen follow a **logical order** (top-to-bottom or left-to-right)?

☐ Can users clearly **undo or exit** actions if they change their mind?

☐ Do actions have immediate **feedback** (loading spinners, success checkmarks, etc.)?

☐ Are **default values** or focus states helping users get started quickly?


## 🧪 Visual Hierarchy

☐ Is the **primary action** (e.g. “Save”, “Next”) visually more prominent?

☐ Are related elements grouped together (labels + fields, actions + context)?

☐ Is whitespace used to separate different sections or steps?

☐ Is everything still understandable without color alone?  
> _Helpful for accessibility and error states._


## 📱 Responsiveness & Devices

☐ Is the experience usable on both desktop and mobile?  
> _Scrollable? No clipped content?_

☐ Are tap targets (buttons/links) easy to hit on mobile?


## 🔁 Error Prevention

☐ Are you validating inputs **before** submission (e.g., email format, required fields)?

☐ Are destructive actions (like "Delete") confirmed or undoable?

☐ Do you prevent duplicate submissions or repeated clicks?


## 🙋‍♀️ Support Readiness

☐ Could a **support team member** or new user understand this screen without training?

☐ Is there a way to recover from common mistakes (e.g., re-enter password, retry upload)?


## ⭐ Final Tip

> “Perfect is the enemy of shipped. But **useful, usable, and understandable** is always within reach.”

Start small. Check a few of these boxes, and your users (and teammates) will thank you.
